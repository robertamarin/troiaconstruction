Why build here

Why build an ADU in San Diego?

San Diego has some of the most ADU-friendly rules in California. The City’s ADU Bonus Program lets qualifying lots in transit priority areas build more than one ADU, turning a single backyard into real rental income or multigenerational space.

We handle the parts that trip people up: load calculations, sewer tie-ins, and Development Services plan check. Because we self-perform the framing, your structure goes up square and on schedule instead of waiting on a rotating cast of subs.

Permitting

Approvals in San Diego.

Permits run through the City of San Diego Development Services Department. Most ADUs are reviewed ministerially on a 60-day clock, and the City’s Bonus ADU Program can unlock additional units on transit-served lots. We prepare the submittal, carry it through plan check, and keep you posted at every milestone.

We handle drawings, submittal, plan-check responses, and inspections, so you get a finished ADU instead of a paperwork project.

San Diego ADU questions

Good to know.

01How many ADUs can I build in San Diego?
On a standard single-family lot you can typically add one ADU plus one junior ADU. Under the City of San Diego’s ADU Bonus Program, lots in transit priority areas may qualify for additional units. We’ll review your parcel and tell you exactly what it allows.
02How long does an ADU take to permit in San Diego?
State law requires ministerial review within 60 days of a complete submittal. Real-world timelines depend on how fast comments are resolved, which is why we manage plan check directly.
03Will an ADU add value in San Diego?
In a rental market this tight, a well-built ADU typically adds both monthly income and resale value. We build to last and keep finishes rent-ready.
