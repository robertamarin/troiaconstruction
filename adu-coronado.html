Why build here

Why build an ADU in Coronado?

Coronado is a small, historic island community where character and detailing matter enormously. ADUs here have to be quiet additions that fit the streetscape. We approach them with the craftsmanship and restraint the island expects.

Space is at a premium, so Coronado ADUs are often refined guest quarters, studios, or above-garage units rather than sprawling builds. We make every square foot work and keep the construction footprint considerate of close neighbors.

Permitting

Approvals in Coronado.

Coronado is its own city with its own review standards, and the island lies within the Coastal Zone. ADU projects here typically involve both city review and coastal considerations. We prepare for Coronado’s specific expectations and manage the process end to end.

We handle drawings, submittal, plan-check responses, and inspections, so you get a finished ADU instead of a paperwork project.

Coronado ADU questions

Good to know.

01Are ADUs allowed on Coronado?
Yes, within California ADU law, though Coronado’s lots and standards shape what fits. We assess your property and design to its constraints.
02Does an ADU on Coronado need coastal review?
Coronado is in the Coastal Zone, so coastal considerations generally apply. We handle that alongside city review.
03Can you build to match a historic Coronado home?
Yes. We match period detailing carefully so the ADU complements the main house and the street.
