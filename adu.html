San Diego ADU Regulations & Permitting Guide

Understanding San Diego ADU rules saves time and money. Here's what every homeowner should know before building an accessory dwelling unit in San Diego County.

ADU Size Limits

Detached ADUs: up to 1,200 sq ft. Attached ADUs: up to 50% of primary dwelling. Junior ADUs (JADUs): up to 500 sq ft within your existing home. San Diego allows both a standard ADU and a JADU on the same lot.

Setback Requirements

Detached ADUs require four-foot side and rear setbacks. Attached ADUs follow primary-structure standards. Garage conversions within existing footprints may have no additional setback requirements under state law.

Parking & Transit

No additional parking required if your property is within half a mile of public transit, within a historic district, or involves a garage conversion. San Diego's extensive transit network means most properties qualify.

ADU Permit Timeline

San Diego targets 60-day plan reviews for ADU applications. State law mandates a 15-business-day completeness check. Coastal zone projects may take longer. We shepherd every submittal and respond to plan check comments fast.

ADU Fee Waivers

San Diego County waives $8,000–$30,000 in impact and connection fees for qualifying ADU projects. We help homeowners identify and apply for every available fee reduction to keep your ADU cost as low as possible.

AB 1033 — Sell Your ADU

San Diego has adopted AB 1033, allowing ADUs to be sold separately as condominiums. We build AB 1033-compliant accessory dwelling units so you have the option to sell your ADU independently from your primary home.
