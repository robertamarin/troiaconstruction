Permitting

Approvals in Escondido.

Escondido is its own city and reviews ADUs through its Building and Planning divisions. It is inland, so no coastal review applies. We prepare the submittal to Escondido’s standards and manage plan check for you.

We handle drawings, submittal, plan-check responses, and inspections, so you get a finished ADU instead of a paperwork project.

Escondido ADU questions

Good to know.

01Are ADUs affordable to build in Escondido?
Escondido’s land values make the overall project more accessible than coastal cities, though construction quality should never be the variable you cut. We build a lasting structure at an honest price.
02Can an ADU house family in Escondido?
Yes, multigenerational use is one of the most common reasons our Escondido clients build. We design for comfort, privacy, and accessibility.
03Does Escondido need a coastal permit?
No. Escondido is inland and outside the Coastal Zone, which keeps the permit path more straightforward.
