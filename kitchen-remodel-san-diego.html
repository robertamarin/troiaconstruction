How we build it

A kitchen is a construction project, not a catalog order.

The best kitchens start with what is behind the walls. Opening up a kitchen often means removing or modifying a wall, and that wall may carry the roof or the floor above. Because Troia self-performs structural framing to engineered plans, beams, headers, and posts are installed correctly instead of being subcontracted out and hoped for.

We coordinate the trades in the right order, electrical for new circuits and lighting, plumbing for relocated sinks and gas lines, ventilation for hoods, so cabinetry and stone go in once and fit. We also help you prioritize where money matters and where a smarter material choice saves real dollars.

Permitting

Permits for kitchen remodels.

Cosmetic updates like new cabinets and counters in the same layout often do not require a full permit, but relocating plumbing, adding circuits, or removing walls usually does. We tell you up front what your scope needs and handle the drawings and inspections.

For older San Diego homes we look for outdated wiring, galvanized supply lines, and undersized panels during the walkthrough, so upgrades are planned rather than discovered after demolition.

Kitchen Remodeling questions

Good to know.

01Can you remove the wall between my kitchen and living room?
Usually, yes. If the wall is load-bearing we work from an engineered design and install a properly sized beam and supports. We confirm what the wall is carrying before demolition.
02Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel in San Diego?
It depends on the scope. Moving plumbing, adding electrical circuits, or changing walls generally requires a permit. Like-for-like cosmetic replacement often does not. We advise you on your specific project.
03Can I stay in my home during the remodel?
Most clients do. We set up dust protection, keep the site clean daily, and help you plan a temporary kitchen setup.
04Do you help with design and material selection?
Yes. We collaborate with you, or with your designer, on layout and materials, and suggest alternatives that hold up well and fit the budget.
