Permitting

Approvals in Chula Vista.

Chula Vista is its own city and reviews ADUs through its Development Services Department. It is inland of the Coastal Zone, so coastal review generally does not apply. We prepare the submittal to Chula Vista standards and manage plan check.

We handle drawings, submittal, plan-check responses, and inspections, so you get a finished ADU instead of a paperwork project.

Chula Vista ADU questions

Good to know.

01Are ADUs allowed in Chula Vista HOAs?
California law limits how much HOAs can block ADUs, though reasonable design standards may apply. We help navigate both the city and any HOA requirements.
02What’s the ADU rental outlook in Chula Vista?
South Bay rental demand is strong and growing. A well-placed ADU typically rents reliably, which we plan for in layout and parking.
03Is Chula Vista in the Coastal Zone?
The developed residential areas are generally inland of the Coastal Zone, which keeps the ADU path more direct.
